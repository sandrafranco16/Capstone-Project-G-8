Our mission

To help individuals advance their AI careers, professionals become more productive, and leaders govern AI confidently.

Through coaching, education, automation and advisory services, from Osborne Park, Western Australia.

Who we are

World-class expertise, within reach.

BITDOT was founded on a simple observation: the people and organisations who need AI guidance most, not-for-profit boards, small businesses, students, career-changers, are the least able to access the expertise large enterprises buy from big consulting firms.

We set out to close that gap. We bring world-class AI, data and governance expertise to your doorstep, so no one is left behind in the AI transition. By listening first and delivering customised solutions, we help individuals and organisations thrive, from a graduate's first AI role to a board's first AI policy.

Our directors bring over 30 years of collective leadership experience, and a habit of doing the work rather than describing it.
