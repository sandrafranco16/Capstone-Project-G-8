Govern AI responsibly · Executives & boards

AI governance & executive education

The problem

Organisations are adopting AI faster than their governance can keep up. Directors carry oversight duties for systems they haven't been trained to question, and policy written for yesterday's technology quietly stops applying. Regulation — including Australia's proposed AI guardrails — is moving whether you are or not.

OutcomeConfident, responsible AI adoption — with accountabilities a regulator would recognise.

We help with

  • AI governance frameworks sized to your organisation
  • AI policy design and accountability structures
  • Executive education and leadership briefings
  • Board services aligned to the AICD Directors' Guide to AI Governance
  • AI strategy development, from ambition to roadmap

Prepare for AI risks · Boards & risk leaders

AI crisis simulation & risk readiness

The problem

AI incidents don't schedule themselves for business hours. The first time your leadership team debates who owns an AI failure shouldn't be while one is trending. Here's how a morning like that unfolds:

  1. 08:42An AI system has generated incorrect customer advice.
  2. 09:15Social media escalation detected — screenshots are circulating.
  3. 09:37Executive team notified. Nobody is sure who owns the response.
  4. 10:05A journalist calls. A regulator's questions arrive by email.
  5. What would your organisation do next?

We help with

  • AI crisis simulations — rehearse the incident before it's real
  • Risk workshops that map your actual exposure
  • Governance advisory for high-risk AI use
  • Regulatory readiness as Australia's guardrails take shape
OutcomeA leadership team that has already made the hard calls once — calmly, in a rehearsal.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Who is BITDOT's career coaching for?

Students, recent graduates and working professionals — including people transitioning into AI roles and AI engineers planning their next step. Coaching covers resumes, LinkedIn, interview preparation, mentoring and a personalised AI engineer roadmap.

Do you work with individuals or organisations?

Both. Individuals typically start with career coaching or the free readiness assessment; organisations engage us for team training, governance advisory, board education and crisis simulations.

Can you provide AI training for whole teams?

Yes. Our applied workshops cover Microsoft Copilot, Claude, prompt engineering, productivity systems and workflow automation — delivered in person or online, in half-day formats, in your real workflows rather than generic examples.

Do you work with boards?

Yes — our board AI competency series is delivered in your boardroom and aligned to the AICD Directors' Guide to AI Governance, with tailored editions for NFP and SMB boards.

What does an AI governance engagement involve?

Typically a governance framework, policy design, executive education and clear accountability structures — sized to your organisation rather than a template, and aligned to Australia's evolving AI guardrails.

How does the AI readiness assessment work?

Six questions, about three minutes. You get your readiness level and matched recommendations immediately, free and ungated. A personalised emailed report is optional — leave your details only if you want it.

Not sure which door is yours?

Take the three-minute assessment and we'll point you at the right one — or skip straight to a discovery call.
