Privacy Policy

BITDOT Consulting Services Pty Ltd ("BITDOT", "we") respects your privacy and handles personal information in accordance with the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

What we collect

  • Name, email address and organisation, when you submit an enquiry or sign up for Automation Lab launch updates.
  • The content of enquiries you send us.
  • Website usage information via analytics tools (Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity), once enabled.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry or booking request.
  • To notify you about the Automation Lab launch, where you have opted in.
  • To understand how the site is used and improve it.

We do not sell personal information. We only share it with service providers necessary to operate this website (for example, hosting, email and booking platforms), under appropriate protections.

The AI readiness assessment

The assessment on our homepage runs entirely in your browser. Your chosen pathway, your answers and your result are not transmitted to us, not stored, and not linked to you. Closing the tab discards them. We collect no contact details through the assessment at all.

Automated decisions

We do not use automated decision-making that could significantly affect your rights or interests. No computer program of ours makes, or substantially assists in making, decisions about you. The readiness assessment returns an indicative self-evaluation for your own use; it is not a decision about you. Should this ever change, we will update this policy in line with the automated decision-making transparency requirements commencing under the Privacy Act on 10 December 2026.

Access, correction and contact

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by emailing info@bitdot.com.au.
